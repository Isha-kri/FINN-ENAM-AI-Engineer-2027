# Third-party source and dependency notice

FINN is a prototype, not a licensed market-data redistribution service.

- Public company reports and transcripts remain copyrighted by their publishers. FINN stores the downloaded PDFs locally for the user's own prototype session and links citations to the original company source. The downloaded files and local database are excluded from Git.
- NSE RSS is the exchange's public syndication source. Review NSE's current site terms before redistribution or production use.
- Yahoo chart history is a third-party convenience source, not an official exchange feed or a grant of rights to republish. Review Yahoo's current terms and use a licensed market feed for production.
- Open-source runtime packages are listed in `package-lock.json` and retain their respective licenses. No package is bundled as a paid service.

No blanket license is asserted over exchange/company source material or market data.
